# Quillkit Canon Index

Canonical documentation for `quillkit`, the one command a quill author runs. Three docs, a bin and the two clients it lays being different subjects:

- [QUILLKIT.md](QUILLKIT.md): the verbs and their targets, the one pin, the gate, the local loop, the deploy layout, and what `App.yaml` resolves to.
- [STUDIO.md](STUDIO.md): the client a quiver's authors judge it in, what it is a client of, the repack loop the document survives, and the preset its chrome is drawn with.
- [APP.md](APP.md): the client the people filling a quiver's forms in reach, the documents it keeps in their browser, and the pins it keeps opening.

The library it carries is [`@quillmark/quiver`'s canon](../../../quiver/prose/canon/INDEX.md); the surfaces the clients mount are [`@quillmark/svelte`'s](../../../svelte/prose/canon/INDEX.md).

The consumer-facing walkthrough with runnable snippets is the package's [`README.md`](../../README.md).
